I have also been getting into geocaching. For those who don't know what that is, you basically are given coordinates (formerly on a gps, now there's an app) which will lead you to the general location of an item. Then you have to find it! This item will usually be somewhere you can sign your name or leave / take a small item. If anyone wants to geocache together soon / this summer shoot me an e-mail.
